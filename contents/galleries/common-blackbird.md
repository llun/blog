The common blackbird is a species of true thrush. It is also known as the Eurasian blackbird (especially in North America, to distinguish it from the distinct New World blackbirds), or simply the blackbird. It breeds in Europe, western Asia, and North Africa, and has been introduced to Australia and New Zealand.
